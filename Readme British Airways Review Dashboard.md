# British Airways Customer Reviews Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
[cite_start]This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing British Airways customer reviews across multiple dimensions such as time, geography, aircraft type, seat class, and service metrics[cite: 3]. [cite_start]The primary goal is to uncover trends in customer satisfaction, identify operational strengths and weaknesses, and provide data-driven insights to support service improvements[cite: 4].

## Key Features & Functionality
* [cite_start]**Interactive Filters:** Users can slice data by Metric (Overall rating, cabin staff, etc.), Date Range (March 2016 – October 2023), Seat Type, Traveler Type, Aircraft Type, and Continent[cite: 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14].
* [cite_start]**Temporal Trends:** A time-series line chart tracks how customer satisfaction fluctuates by month, highlighting periods of disruption or improvement[cite: 16, 17].
* [cite_start]**Geographical Mapping:** A world map visualizes regional service quality, helping identify specific routes with high or low satisfaction levels[cite: 19, 20].
* [cite_start]**Aircraft Performance:** Bar charts compare ratings across different aircraft models, balanced by review volume to ensure statistical context[cite: 21, 24].

## Key Insights for Recruiters
* [cite_start]**Critical Service Gaps:** Entertainment (1.4) and Food/Beverages (2.4) are the primary drivers of negative sentiment[cite: 34, 37].
* **Fleet Variance:** High-performing aircraft like the **Boeing 747-400 (4.7)** and **Boeing 787 (4.4)** significantly outperform narrow-body models like the **A321 (3.6)**.
* [cite_start]**Frontline Performance:** Despite physical product challenges, cabin staff service remains a brand differentiator with a higher relative score of 3.3[cite: 40, 58].

## Strategic Recommendations
* [cite_start]**Modernize IFE:** Prioritize fleet-wide upgrades for In-Flight Entertainment (IFE) systems to address the lowest-rated metric[cite: 46, 47].
* [cite_start]**Economy Refurbishment:** Focus on seat ergonomics and legroom optimization to improve perceived value for money[cite: 52, 53, 54].
* [cite_start]**Route-Level Monitoring:** Investigate regions with consistently lower ratings to implement targeted operational interventions[cite: 66, 67].
